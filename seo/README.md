# SEO Skills

Skills for search engine optimization: research, content planning, and visibility in Google and AI answers.

**Belongs here:** skills whose output helps a page rank or get cited, such as keyword and question research, content briefs, and audits. Paid search work goes in [google-ads](../google-ads/).

## Skills

| Skill | What it does | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| [seo-people-also-ask](seo-people-also-ask/) | Searches Google in your Chrome through Claude in Chrome and returns the "People also ask" questions and "People also search for" terms for a search term. | [Guide](../guides/seo/seo-people-also-ask.md) |
