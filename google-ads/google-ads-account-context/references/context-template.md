# Google Ads account context template

Structure adapted from the product marketing context document (and its Paid Ads section) in [coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) (MIT, Copyright (c) 2025 Corey Haines).

Copy everything below the line into `.agents/google-ads-context.md`. Keep the `##` section names exactly as written, because other Google Ads skills find sections by name: the validator in google-ads-apply-changes reads "Protected terms" and the three "Max ..." lines in "Permissions and limits". Replace the bracketed hints. Write MISSING for anything unknown. Delete a hint line only when its section truly doesn't apply, and say "not applicable" instead of leaving it blank.
